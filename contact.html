Frequently Asked Questions

Is Food Shazam free to use?

Yes! Food Shazam is completely free. Simply upload a photo of any food and our AI will identify it instantly.

How accurate is the food recognition?

Our AI can identify thousands of foods, dishes, and ingredients from around the world with high accuracy. Results may vary for very niche or regional dishes.

Do you store my photos?

No. Your uploaded photos are processed in real time and are never stored on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I report a wrong identification?

Simply email us with the food photo and the incorrect result. We use this feedback to improve the app.
