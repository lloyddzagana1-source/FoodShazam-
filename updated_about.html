About FoodShazam

We're on a mission to bridge the gap between your plate and your health goals through the power of Artificial Intelligence.

Our Vision

FoodShazam was founded with a simple yet ambitious goal: to make nutrition transparent and accessible for everyone. We believe that understanding what you eat shouldn't require a degree in dietetics or hours of manual data entry.

"Our AI puts a world-class nutritionist in everyone's pocket. By simply snapping a photo, we empower users to make informed decisions about their health in real-time."

Whether you're managing a specific diet, trying to lose weight, or simply curious about a new dish at a restaurant, FoodShazam provides instant, science-backed insights.

Why We Built This

The founder, Lloyd Dzagana, realized that the biggest barrier to healthy eating wasn't a lack of will, but a lack of convenient information. Traditional calorie trackers are tedious, leading to high dropout rates. FoodShazam solves this by making tracking as easy as taking a photo.
