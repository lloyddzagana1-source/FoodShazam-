Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 3, 2026

At FoodShazam, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and AI food recognition tools.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • User-Uploaded Images: When you use our food recognition tool, we process the images you upload. These images are used solely for identification and are not stored permanently on our servers.
  • Usage Data: We may collect information about how you interact with our site, such as pages visited and features used.
  • Device Information: Basic information about your browser, device type, and IP address to ensure technical stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide instant food identification and nutritional data.
  • Improve our AI models and website performance.
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide support.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the site may not function correctly.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network privacy policy.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party sites (such as affiliate partners). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external websites.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: foodshazam@gmail.com
