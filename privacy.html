Welcome to Food Shazam ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your information. 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use Food Shazam:

  • Food photos you upload: Images you submit for food identification are processed in real-time by our AI. We do not permanently store your food photos on our servers.
  • Usage data: We may automatically collect information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent on the site, and browser type.
  • Device information: Basic information such as your device type, operating system, and screen resolution may be collected to help us improve the user experience.
  • Cookies and tracking data: We use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience. See Section 5 for full details.

📸 Your food photos are sent to AI processing services for identification only. They are not sold, shared with third parties for marketing, or used to train AI models without your explicit consent.

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • To provide food identification and nutritional information services
  • To improve the accuracy and performance of our AI recognition technology
  • To analyze website usage and improve user experience
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • To comply with legal obligations

Google AdSense uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to serve ads based on your browsing history and interests. This is known as interest-based advertising or personalized advertising.

  • Affiliate cookies: Used by our affiliate partners (HelloFresh, Amazon, Instacart) to track referrals when you click links on our site.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website and the relevance of advertisements shown to you.
