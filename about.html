About Food Shazam

We're on a mission to help everyone understand what they eat — one photo at a time.

Our Mission

Food Shazam was born from a simple idea: you should be able to point your phone at any food and instantly know what it is, what's in it, and how to make it yourself.

"We believe that knowledge about food is a right, not a privilege. Our AI puts a world-class nutritionist and chef in everyone's pocket — for free."

Whether you're traveling and curious about local cuisine, trying to track your nutrition, or just spotted something delicious on someone else's plate — Food Shazam gives you the answer instantly.
